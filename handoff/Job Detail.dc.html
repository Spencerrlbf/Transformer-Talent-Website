Open linked · Northline Search

Edit Close job
About the role

Northline's client runs payments infrastructure for mid-market lenders. They're hiring a senior backend engineer to own the settlement path end to end — correctness first, throughput second. Small team, no layers between you and the problem.

Responsibilities
—Own settlement and reconciliation services in production
—Move the ledger from nightly batch to event-driven writes
—Carry on-call for the payments critical path, one week in five
—Mentor two mid-level engineers without becoming a manager
Requirements
—5+ years on backend systems where money or state correctness mattered
—Production Go or Java, and real PostgreSQL depth
—Has run an event-driven system, not just read about one
Nice to have
—Payments, ledgers or clearing background
—Kafka or an equivalent log
Visa

Sponsorship available for the right candidate; existing right to work preferred.
