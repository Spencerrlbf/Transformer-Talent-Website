Notes

All three keep the current hero claim, the sub-paragraph, the investor line and the two audience CTAs verbatim from app/page.tsx. The chrome is grounded in app/layout.tsx: the Transformer_Talent underscore wordmark, the path-style nav (/about · /market-index · /roles) with UPLOAD JD →, the SYSTEM LIVE status and the real footer with the slogan and Spencer’s address. Two deliberate drops: the terminal-window hero block (the strongest piece of the old dark identity, with no equivalent in this language) and the invented “For companies” nav item, which was never in the real topbar.

Placements are the real five from data/placements.ts — Fleet AI, Ironsite, Virtualitics, Adaptive, Palantir — with their tags and, in 11c, the one-line story each carries. Each links out to the company as it does today. The stats in 11b and 11c are all derived from data you already have (roles count, market-index.json); “48h reply” comes from the apply and JD flows — confirm it’s a promise you want on the homepage.

11c implies product work the site doesn’t have yet: the inline JD box needs the TalentMatcher flow lifted onto the homepage, and the “what comes back” preview is a promise about output quality. 11a and 11b are pure restyles of what exists.
