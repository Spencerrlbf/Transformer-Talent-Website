About the role

The fleet planner decides what every robot in a building does next. You will own it end to end: the solver, the simulation harness it is tested against, and the rollout to two hundred live sites. It is the highest-leverage system the company runs and it cannot go down.

What you’ll do

—Own the planner service — solver, replanning loop, and the failure paths when a robot drops off the network mid-task.
—Build the simulation harness the team ships against; every change is proven against a week of recorded floor traffic first.

—Six or more years on systems where correctness under load matters — scheduling, routing, trading, telemetry.

—Experience owning a service in production, on call for it, and improving it rather than rewriting it.
Nice to have
—Robotics, warehouse or logistics background.
—You have written a simulator you actually trusted.

For implementation

One RolesTable serves both /roles (showSelectionUI true — spec banner, side rail, mobile bar) and /apply (false — table only, the external rail owns the cart). Selection is the shared applySelection localStorage module, capped at 3, synced via its change subscription — that is why the count is identical on all three views here. /apply?role=1042 preselects one role; /apply?speculative=1 hides the table entirely and makes the resume required.

Search semantics are unchanged and worth preserving: comma = OR groups, spaces = AND, -term excludes, “quoted phrase” is literal. Sort keys stay id · title · location · workplace · yoe · salary, 25 per page. Role detail keeps its JobPosting JSON-LD and the two fact tables — the confidential-company framing (“named during the process”) is the commercial rule, not placeholder copy.

Changed from the current site: Office and Experience fold into the role cell’s meta line (five columns, no horizontal scroll at desktop width), the four filter selects become the Filters menu + chips used by every table in this system, and role detail gains an explicit “One application covers up to 3 roles” line next to the CTA.

The rail is ApplyForm, deliberately self-contained (roles in, one endpoint out) — two panels, Your application then Your details, with the intro line changing by state. Cart titles link to /roles/[slug] in a new tab, which is why each role is passed its slug. Submit posts FormData to /api/apply with roleIds (plus speculative=1); on success it resets the form, calls clearSelection() and replaces the whole rail with the thank-you. Six states sit in the review switcher: idle, sending, the client-side missing-resume error, a network/server error, submitted (roles named back), and alreadyApplied. No suggested-matches list here — that belongs to the tenant board, not /apply.

Selection is applySelection.ts: localStorage key tt-apply-roles, capped at MAX_ROLES = 3, broadcasting tt-apply-roles-changed for same-tab sync and listening to storage for cross-tab. A fourth add is a silent no-op — the n/3 counter is what explains it, so keep the counter visible wherever APPLY + appears. ?role= merges into the stored selection rather than replacing it.
