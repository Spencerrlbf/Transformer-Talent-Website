How we hire

The rounds we draw from.

The exact steps vary by role; your recruiter confirms the process up front. Click a round for the full detail.

1 Recruiter call what you want next, honestly 30 min
A conversation, not a screen. We cover the role, the comp band, the team you would join and what would make you say no. You leave knowing whether to continue.
2 Technical deep dive your work, not a puzzle 75 min
Bring a system you built. Two engineers go deep on the decisions you made and what you would change. No live coding, no take-home.
3 Site day a real building, a real fleet half day
You spend a morning at a customer site with the team, then meet your future manager and one founder. Expenses covered, remote alternative available.

Offers go out within 48 hours of the site day. We never ask for unpaid work and we will tell you where you stand at every step.
